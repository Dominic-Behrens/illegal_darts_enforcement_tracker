    About this data

    This is an independent view of the NSW Health tobacco closure-order register, not an official NSW Health service.

    What the snapshots mean

    “First observed” means an ID appeared in this tracker for the first time, not that an order was issued that week. The first snapshot is a baseline and is excluded from these counts. Snapshots show what the register listed at the time, not a complete enforcement history.

    One download, not a history

    This preview contains only one download of the public register. It cannot establish when an order first appeared or how the register changed over time.

    Archived captures are sparse

    Capture dates identify when a copy of the register was available in the Internet Archive; the latest date is a live CSV download. Dates without captures are unknown, not zero. These observations cannot establish when orders first appeared or how many enforcement actions occurred between captures.

    Listed does not mean active today

    Counts describe rows in the selected download. The register omits expired orders; a disappearance alone cannot establish when or why an order ended. Published commencement and conclusion dates are order dates, not observation dates.

    Locations and records

    Map points use NSW Health’s published coordinates, without geocoding or correction. Some rows have no coordinates. The source has no stable record ID; changes to a premise, address or commencement date may appear as separate records in the tracker.
